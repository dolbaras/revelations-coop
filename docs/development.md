# Development notes (Resident Evil Revelations Co-op)

Developer material that is not for players: layout, test variables, console words. The players' text is `README.md` and `docs/release-readme.txt` (shipped as `README-COOP.txt`); the release protocol is `docs/releasing.md`.

- **Developer tools** (do nothing in a player's game): `ulist [g<k>|name]` lists every group of the unit table (enemies are in group 13, `units` shows only group 10), `tp <jill|parker|hexunit> x y z` teleports in one frame like the story's own set-position op, `split fov <degrees>` (experiment), `warp <91EESS00>` (`native/warp.c`) starts any story section through the stock Continue (Episode 2 section 1 with Chris and Jessica ran in split screen on the lab PC); `REV1_PADS=1` lets virtual test pads reach a background test instance (a private copy of XInput bypasses the Steam overlay's Steam Input hook).
- `native/steam.c`: logs the game's Steam calls; `REV1_ACHBLOCK=1` (set by the lab scripts) blocks achievement / stat writes in test runs.
Layout: `native/` proxy (C, 32-bit, built with Zig: `sh native/build.sh`; hot-loadable experiment plug-ins `plug_*.c`: `build_plug.sh`, `build_split.sh`), `tools/r1console.py` console client.
Variables: `REV1_LOG=<file>`, `REV1_CONSOLE=<port>` (UDP on 127.0.0.1), `REV1_ANYEXE=1` (lab only), `REV1_SPLIT=v|h` (start with the split screen requested, side by side / top-bottom), `REV1_MENU=0` (switch the Co-Op Play entry off), `REV1_PADDEDUP=0` (no duplicate-pad detection), `REV1_NATIVEROW=0` (no fourth row in the main menu: hint + F6 / BACK only), `REV1_LANG=<code>` (language of the lobby texts), `REV1_COOP_INI=<file>` (settings file),
test-window variables of the lab (`REV1_WINPOS`, `REV1_WINSIZE`, `REV1_BACKGROUND`, `REV1_MUTE`, `REV1_ADAPTER`).
Console words (UDP): `menu status | open | close | view | set | gm`, `split status | on [v|h] | off | lay v|h | hud | menu | view | probe`, `splitkill` (emergency off from any thread), `cap status | bind | own | src | ...`.
Give to partner (RE5 style, `native/give1.c`, `give1.h`, notes `notes/give1.md`): in a co-op session (split screen or network) the stock item screen (BACK) shows '<B_DE> Give to partner' on a herb, grenade or ammo cell while the partner is up and within 3 m; decide gives one herb / grenade or half of the ammo (network: session event 0x144 GIVE / ACK / CANCEL, refund by the receiver's answer). `REV1_GIVE=0` = off. Console: `give [status] | on | off | gui | bag [slot] | lab <slot> <tab> <id hex>` (LAB: the screen's give without the screen) `| fill <slot> herb|ammo|gren <index> <n>` (LAB: the stock add) `| set reach <u> | sound <ok> <err>`.

The Genesis for the second player (`native/scan1.c`, `scan1.h`, notes `notes/scan1.md`): every human of the co-op game gets a Genesis as soon as one has it (the story gives it to one character only); in split screen the second human uses it in the whole view as the own human (the menu exchange of `split.c`, kind 3; one scanner, first come first served); his own analysis rate and herb outside 1-2 (1-2 keeps the team rate of the story); network: a hidden item revealed on one PC is revealed on the other (session event 0x145). `REV1_SCAN=0` = off. Console: `scan [status] | who | items | on | off | x 0|1 | give | p2rate <n>` (LAB) `| reveal <item hex>` (LAB: the stock scan result).

Hunk (a second human where the story has no partner for him: the Guest Cabin part 2-2 locks Parker away; `native/hunk1.h`): default (no variable): Hunk instead of the story-locked Parker of 2-2 only, network and split screen (`HK_PRODUCT`, `hunk_mode()`; the lab took 2-2 with Hunk to its end and into 3-1 on two PCs; in split screen the script's FsmLockPlayer partner lock lands on the second human and is taken off with the stock unlock FUN_006AE390: `bag1_game.inc` hunk_split_unlock); `REV1_LAB_HUNK=0` switches it off (the second player watches 2-2), `REV1_LAB_HUNK=1|2|3` are lab modes (1 = only 16-2 of episode 16, the hidden TGS demo episode the campaign never reaches; 2 = in every paired section; 3 = 2-2 and 16-1), `REV1_LAB_NOSPEC=1` (lab: the watching rule off). Console: `world pcls [slot hex]` / `world pcls persist <slot> <hex>|off` (the player class ids the next level start builds its humans from), `world plist`, `world pparam [id]`, `world hpbp [keep|off]`, `world set fallres 1`, `packcopy <from> <to>` / `packcopy undo`, `barrier gflags set` (the section-end flags: the stock end flow goes on to the next section).
